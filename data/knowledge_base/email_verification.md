# Unable to Register or Verify Email

## Problem Description
A client is unable to complete the registration process or verify their email address on the Operation HOPE Client Portal. This may involve not receiving the verification code or encountering errors during password creation.

## Resolution Steps

1. Navigate to the Operation HOPE Client Portal
2. Click the **Register** button
3. Enter the client's email address in the registration form
4. Check the email inbox for a verification code
   - If the code is not in the inbox, check the **Spam** or **Junk** folder
   - The verification email is sent from the Operation HOPE portal system
5. Enter the verification code in the portal when prompted
6. Create a password that meets the following requirements:
   - Minimum 8 characters
   - At least one uppercase letter
   - At least one lowercase letter
   - At least one special character (e.g., !, @, #, $)
   - At least one number

## Response Template

"To complete your registration, please follow these steps: Go to the Operation HOPE Client Portal and click Register. Enter your email address, then check your inbox (and spam/junk folder) for the verification code. Enter the code when prompted, and create a password with at least 8 characters including uppercase, lowercase, a special character, and a number. Let us know if you need further assistance."

## Follow-Up Questions

- Did you receive the verification email? If not, have you checked your spam or junk folder?
- Are you seeing any specific error messages during the registration process?
- Which email provider are you using (Gmail, Outlook, Yahoo, etc.)?

## Internal Notes

- Some email providers may delay delivery of the verification code by several minutes.
- If the client repeatedly does not receive the verification email, verify that the email address is correctly spelled and check Azure AD for any existing entries that may conflict.
- Corporate or organizational email filters may block the verification email. Suggest using a personal email address as an alternative.
