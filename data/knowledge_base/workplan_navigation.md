# Navigating Workplans on the Client Portal

## Problem Description
A client needs guidance on how to navigate to and interact with workplans on the Operation HOPE Client Portal. Workplans contain activities and tasks related to the client's enrolled programs.

## Resolution Steps

1. **Log in to the Operation HOPE Client Portal** using your registered email and password
2. **Navigate to your Programs section** from the main dashboard
3. **Select the program** you are enrolled in (e.g., Homeownership, Credit & Money Management)
4. **Click on the Workplan tab** within the selected program
5. **Review available tasks and activities** listed in the workplan
6. **Select a task** to view its details and instructions
7. **Complete the task** by following the on-screen prompts
8. **Mark the task as complete** when finished (if applicable)
9. **Repeat for additional tasks** in the workplan

**Note:** All tasks in the workplan are optional but recommended. Completing workplan activities helps you get the most out of your program and track your progress toward your goals.

## Response Template

"To navigate your workplan on the Client Portal, log in and go to your Programs section. Select the program you are enrolled in, then click on the Workplan tab. You will see a list of tasks and activities. Click on any task to view the details and complete it. Please note that all tasks are optional but recommended to help you achieve your goals. We have also attached a visual guide (Client Portal Navigation for Workplan Activities) for your reference. Let us know if you need further assistance."

## Internal Notes

- A PDF visual guide titled "Client Portal Navigation for Workplan Activities" is available as an attachment for clients who need step-by-step screenshots.
- Workplan tasks are not mandatory. Clients should not be told they must complete them.
- If a client reports that their workplan is empty, verify their program enrollment status in Dynamics365.
- Workplans are assigned by coaches. If no workplan exists, the coach may need to create one for the client.
