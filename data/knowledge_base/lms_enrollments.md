# LMS Course Enrollments or Completion Tracking Issues

## Problem Description
A client reports issues with LMS course enrollments or completion tracking. Courses may appear incomplete in the portal even when they have been finished, or enrollment status may not display correctly.

## Resolution Steps

1. **Check the client's LMS enrollments**
   - Log into the LMS administration panel
   - Search for the client by email or name
   - Review the client's course enrollments

2. **Verify completion status**
   - Confirm that courses are marked as 100% completed in the system
   - Check for any sync delays between LMS and the client portal

3. **If courses show 100% in LMS but not in portal:**
   - This is typically a display/cache issue
   - Inform the client the data is correct in the system
   - The display should resolve on its own (often within 24 hours)

4. **If courses are NOT 100% in LMS:**
   - Identify which lessons or modules are incomplete
   - Provide guidance to the client on completing the remaining content
   - Escalate if there is a technical issue preventing completion

## Response Template

"I've reviewed your LMS enrollments, and I can confirm that your courses are marked as 100% completed in the system. If you're still seeing them in your portal, it's likely due to a display issue that should resolve on its own."

## Internal Notes

- LMS and client portal may have sync delays; allow up to 24 hours for display updates.
- Always verify completion status in the LMS before responding to the client.
- Document any recurring sync issues for escalation to the development team.
