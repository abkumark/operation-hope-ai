# Course Feedback - Spelling, Content Errors, or Suggestions

## Problem Description
A client has submitted feedback about a course, including spelling errors, content inaccuracies, or suggestions for improvement. This requires acknowledging the feedback and gathering specifics to route to the content team for review and correction.

## Resolution Steps

1. **Acknowledge the feedback**
   - Thank the client for taking the time to provide feedback
   - Confirm that their input is valued and will be reviewed

2. **Gather specific details**
   - Ask for the specific module or section where the issue was noticed
   - Request the exact text or location if possible (e.g., lesson name, slide number)
   - For suggestions, ask for a brief description of the proposed improvement

3. **Create internal ticket**
   - Log the feedback in the appropriate system for L&D/content team review
   - Include: course name, module/section, type of issue (spelling/content/suggestion), and client description

4. **Set expectations**
   - Inform the client that the content team will review and correct as appropriate
   - Do not promise a specific timeline unless one is established

## Response Template

"Thank you for your feedback on the course content. Could you let us know the specific module or section where you noticed the issue? We'll review and correct it promptly. Your input helps us improve the learning experience for everyone!"

## Internal Notes

- All course feedback should be logged for L&D review, even if minor.
- Prioritize spelling and factual errors over stylistic suggestions.
- Track recurring feedback on the same content for prioritization.
