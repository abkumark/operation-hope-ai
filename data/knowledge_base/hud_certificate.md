# HUD Certification Through Online Learning

## Problem Description
A client needs to obtain HUD Certification through the Operation HOPE online learning platform. The certification requires completing a specific sequence of courses and following the certification process at the end.

## Resolution Steps

1. **Register for the Homeownership Program**
   - Ensure the client is enrolled in the Homeownership Program

2. **Set goal to "Buy a Home"**
   - Navigate to the client's goals and set the primary goal to "Buy a Home"

3. **Complete the required courses in order:**
   - Before You Buy
   - Purchasing Your Home
   - After You Buy

4. **Access the certification**
   - At the end of the last lesson, click the certification link
   - Complete the short survey
   - Find the Certification icon in the top-left of the screen
   - Download the PDF or print the certificate

5. **Verify completion**
   - Confirm all three courses show 100% completion before accessing the certification

## Response Template

"To obtain your HUD Certification, please follow these steps: 1) Register for the Homeownership Program and set your goal to 'Buy a Home.' 2) Complete the three required courses in order: Before You Buy, Purchasing Your Home, and After You Buy. 3) At the end of the last lesson, click the certification link and complete the short survey. 4) Find the Certification icon in the top-left corner to download your PDF or print your certificate. Let us know if you need any assistance along the way."

## Internal Notes

- Courses must be completed in the specified order for certification eligibility.
- The certification link only appears after all three courses are 100% complete.
- Clients may need to refresh or log out and back in if the certification icon does not appear immediately.
