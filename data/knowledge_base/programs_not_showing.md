# Programs, Workplan, or Goals Not Showing

## Problem Description
A client reports that their programs, workplan, or goals are not visible on the Operation HOPE Client Portal. This is most commonly caused by browser cache issues or an outdated browser.

## Resolution Steps

1. **Clear browser cache:**
   - In Chrome: Three dots menu > More Tools > Clear Browsing Data > Cached images and files > Clear
   - In Safari: Develop menu > Empty Caches
   - In Firefox: Settings > Privacy & Security > Clear Data > Cached Web Content
   - In Edge: Settings > Privacy, Search, and Services > Clear Browsing Data

2. **Try Incognito or Private Browsing mode:**
   - Chrome: `Ctrl+Shift+N` (Windows) or `Cmd+Shift+N` (Mac)
   - Safari: File > New Private Window
   - Firefox: `Ctrl+Shift+P` (Windows) or `Cmd+Shift+P` (Mac)

3. **Make sure your browser is up to date:**
   - Check for browser updates in the browser settings or help menu
   - Restart the browser after updating

4. **Try a different browser:**
   - If the issue persists, switch to a different browser (Chrome, Firefox, Edge, or Safari)

5. **If the issue persists after all steps:**
   - Contact support with a screenshot of what you see on the portal
   - Include the browser name and version you are using

## Response Template

"We understand your programs, workplan, or goals are not showing on the portal. Please try the following: Clear your browser cache, then try opening the portal in an incognito or private browsing window. Make sure your browser is up to date, and if the issue continues, try using a different browser. If the problem persists after these steps, please send us a screenshot of what you see so we can investigate further."

## Internal Notes

- If the issue persists after browser troubleshooting, check the client's program enrollment in Dynamics365 to verify they are actively enrolled.
- Missing programs may indicate the client has not been enrolled by their coach yet. Contact the assigned coach to verify.
- Missing workplans may need to be created or assigned by the coach in Dynamics365.
- Missing goals may indicate the client has not set goals yet or the coach has not configured them.
